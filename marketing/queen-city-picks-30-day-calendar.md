## Weekly Operating Rhythm

- **Monday:** Publish the verified seven-day roundup. Link to the site's events calendar, not a stale social-only list.
- **Wednesday:** Publish one venue, artist, neighborhood, or community feature. Rotate genres and Cincinnati/NKY geography.
- **Friday:** Publish a practical weekend guide with official ticket links and a clear affiliate disclosure wherever a link may earn commission.
- **Sunday:** Invite community replies, recommendations, and permission-cleared attendee content. Keep this optional if moderation time is limited.

## Editorial Guardrails

- Aim for genre balance across each two-week period: country/Americana, alternative/indie, rock, hip-hop/R&B, Latin, comedy, electronic, and community/cultural events. Balance actual inventory rather than forcing a genre into a week with no confirmed listings.
- Use published event records and official venue, organizer, artist, or ticket pages as the source of truth. Check ticket links immediately before scheduling and again before posting.
- Exclude Madison Theater / Madison Live while it is closed and retired from the active venue roster.
- Keep bilingual copy English-first unless the specific artist, event, or audience makes Spanish useful. Avoid framing the whole platform as Latin-focused.
- Never imply a partnership, endorsement, booking, sold-out status, or ticket availability without confirmation.
- For affiliate links, disclose the relationship near the link. If a ticket URL is not a tracked affiliate link, don't claim that it is.

## Lightweight Measurement

Record weekly totals for site sessions, event-page visits, outbound ticket clicks, event submissions, partner inquiries, social saves/shares, and email signups if an email signup flow is active. Compare click-through by post format and genre after four weeks; use the result to adjust the next calendar rather than treating one post's reach as demand.
